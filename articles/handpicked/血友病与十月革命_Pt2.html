血友病B与十月革命的关系 (Pt.2)

The relationship between hemophilia B and the Great October Revolution

Author: Espin von Linné

伟大的垃圾文章科学家（三中实验部）

【评级：精选 A类垃圾】

大爆发 The Explosion

By July 1917, a failed offensive against Germany sparked massive protests in Petrograd. The Bolsheviks briefly tried to seize power but were suppressed; Lenin fled to Finland. In August, General Kornilov attempted a military coup against Kerensky. The Bolsheviks were armed to defend the capital. The coup failed, and the Bolsheviks' prestige soared.

到1917年7月，对德国攻势的失败引发了彼得格勒的大规模抗议。布尔什维克短暂试图夺权但被镇压；列宁逃往芬兰。8月，科尔尼洛夫将军企图对克伦斯基发动军事政变。布尔什维克被武装起来保卫首都。政变失败，布尔什维克的声望急剧飙升。

On the night of October 24 (Julian), Bolshevik Red Guards and revolutionary soldiers began occupying key points in Petrograd. At 9:45 PM, the cruiser Aurora fired a blank shot, and at 10:00 PM, the Bolsheviks launched an assault on the Winter Palace. By 2:00 AM on October 26, the ministers were arrested. The Bolsheviks had seized power with remarkably little bloodshed.

10月24日（儒略历）晚，布尔什维克赤卫队和革命士兵开始占领彼得格勒的要点。晚上9点45分，阿芙乐尔号巡洋舰鸣放空炮，10点，布尔什维克对冬宫发起进攻。到10月26日凌晨2点，部长们被捕。布尔什维克以极少的流血夺取了政权。

Later that night, the Second All-Russian Congress of Soviets convened, issuing the Decree on Peace and the Decree on Land. A new government, the Council of People's Commissars, was formed with Lenin as its head. Over the next three years, Russia descended into a brutal civil war between the Bolsheviks (Reds) and their opponents (Whites).

当晚，第二次全俄苏维埃代表大会召开，通过了《和平法令》和《土地法令》。以列宁为首的人民委员会成立。在接下来的三年里，俄国陷入了布尔什维克（红军）与其反对者（白军）之间残酷的内战。
