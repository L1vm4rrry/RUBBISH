血友病B与十月革命的关系 (Pt.1)

The relationship between hemophilia B and the Great October Revolution

Author: Espin von Linné

伟大的垃圾文章科学家（三中实验部）

【评级：精选 A类垃圾】

引言 Introduction

The hemophilia disease, is a common disease caused by the mutation of the alleles VIII and IX(sometimes also XI). Its common symptoms include slow blood coagulation and massive bleeding. The typical hemophilia caused by allele IX is commonly referred as “Royal hemophilia”(Hemophilia B) Of which had pestered the clan of European Monarchs for generations, One of the most famous victims of this disease is Alexei Nikolaevich Romanov, son of Tzar Nicholas II. He was killed in the October Revolution, yet though unlikely, does his genetic disease somehow caused the great Revolution? This paper deems to find out.

血友病是一种由等位基因VIII和IX（有时也有XI）突变引起的常见疾病。其常见症状包括血液凝固缓慢和大出血。由等位基因IX引起的典型血友病通常被称为“皇家血友病”（血友病B），它困扰了欧洲君主家族数代人。这种疾病最著名的受害者之一是沙皇尼古拉二世的儿子阿列克谢·尼古拉耶维奇·罗曼诺夫。他死于十月革命，然而虽然看似牵强，但这份遗传疾病是否在某种程度上引发了那场伟大的革命？本文试图找出答案。

血友病B的本质 The Nature of Hemophilia B

Hemophilia B is caused by a mutation in the F9 gene, located on the X chromosome, which provides instructions for making clotting factor IX. In 2009, DNA analysis of the Romanov family's remains revealed the exact mutation: a single change in the F9 gene (IVS3-3A>G) that disrupts how the gene's instructions are read. This results in a severely truncated, nonfunctional version of factor IX.

血友病B是由位于X染色体上的F9基因突变引起的，该基因提供制造凝血因子IX的指令。2009年，对罗曼诺夫家族遗骸的DNA分析揭示了确切的突变：F9基因的一个单核苷酸变化（IVS3-3A>G），破坏了基因指令的读取方式，导致产生严重截短、无功能的因子IX版本。

As an X-linked recessive disorder, the inheritance pattern explains why it primarily affected males: Males (XY) have one mutated X chromosome and cause the disease; Females (XX) have one mutated X making them carriers, typically without symptoms.

作为一种X连锁隐性遗传病，其遗传模式解释了为什么它主要影响男性：男性（XY）有一个突变的X染色体就会患病；女性（XX）有一个突变的X成为携带者，通常没有症状。

十月革命的进程 (前奏) The Prelude to the Revolution

Russia entered World War I in 1914 with a weak economy and an autocratic monarchy. By 1917, the war had caused massive military defeats, food shortages, and widespread discontent. In February 1917 (Julian), spontaneous strikes and riots in Petrograd forced Tsar Nicholas II to abdicate, ending centuries of Romanov rule.

俄国在1914年以疲弱的经济和专制君主制进入第一次世界大战。到1917年，战争造成了大规模的军事失败、粮食短缺和普遍不满。1917年2月（儒略历），彼得格勒自发的罢工和骚乱迫使沙皇尼古拉二世退位，结束了罗曼诺夫家族几个世纪的统治。
