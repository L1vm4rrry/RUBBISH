湖心亭看雪（英语胡乱手译版）

原文（张岱《湖心亭看雪》）

崇祯五年十二月，余住西湖。大雪三日，湖中人鸟声俱绝。是日更定矣，余拏一小舟，拥毳衣炉火，独往湖心亭看雪。雾凇沆砀，天与云与山与水，上下一白。湖上影子，惟长堤一痕、湖心亭一点、与余舟一芥、舟中人两三粒而已。

到亭上，有两人铺毡对坐，一童子烧酒炉正沸。见余，大喜曰："湖中焉得更有此人！"拉余同饮。余强饮三大白而别。问其姓氏，是金陵人，客此。及下船，舟子喃喃曰："莫说相公痴，更有痴似相公者！"

英语胡乱手译版（Watching Snow at The Lake-center Pavilion）

Watching Snow at The Lake‑center Pavilion
Zhang Dai of Ming

The time being the fifth December of Chongzhen, staying at the West Lake. Three days had the snow been falling, and in the lake sound of both man and bird had gone extinct. At eight that night, rowing a small boat, with furcoat and stovefire , alone I went to watch the snow at the Lake‑center Pavilion. Rime and frost filled the air, the sky and clouds and mountains and water all immersed in white, the only shapes upon the lake: a trace of the Causeway, a dot of the Pavilion, and a blade of my ship, some grains of the men.

At the Pavilion, two man sat opposite on a rug. A child boiling the winepot. Upon seeing me they exclaimed, "How can such a man be here in the lake!" and welcomed me to drink. I drank three cupfuls and departed. They being Jinling‘i, also sojourners. When I left the ship, the boatman murmured, "Obsessed as you are , there're even ones obsessed like you."
