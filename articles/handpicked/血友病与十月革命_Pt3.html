血友病B与十月革命的关系 (Pt.3)

The relationship between hemophilia B and the Great October Revolution

Author: Espin von Linné

伟大的垃圾文章科学家（三中实验部）

【评级：精选 A类垃圾】

“神圣”纽带：血友病与拉斯普京 The Connection

However, what do this have to do with prince Alexei? Alexei suffered from Hemophilia B by a very small age. His father, trying to save his heir to the throne, in his despair, he invited the holy man Grigori Rasputin to try to help his son.

然而，这一切又和阿列克谢王子有什么关系呢？阿列克谢从小就患有血友病B。他的父亲为了挽救继承人，在绝望中邀请了神人格里高利·拉斯普京来帮助他的儿子。

Between 1915 and 1916, Rasputin, backed by the Empress, frequently interfered in cabinet appointments—a period known as the "ministerial leapfrog". In just two years, he caused the dismissal of four Prime Ministers, six Ministers of the Interior, and four War Ministers. Notably, he replaced the Minister of Railways with a personal loyalist who knew nothing about rail logistics, paralyzing the Trans‑Siberian Railway.

1915年至1916年间，拉斯普京在皇后的支持下频繁干涉内阁任命——这段时间被称为“大臣跳背游戏”。短短两年内，他导致了四位总理、六位内政大臣和四位战争大臣的下台。尤其值得注意的是，他用自己的亲信替换了交通大臣，而此人完全不懂铁路物流，直接导致西伯利亚大铁路瘫痪。

Impact on the proletariat: The railway paralysis disrupted the supply of fuel and food. By the winter of 1916–1917, factories in Petrograd shut down due to lack of coal, causing massive unemployment. City bread supplies were cut off. Workers had to stand in queues 5 to 6 hours in –20°C weather, only to receive black bread mixed with sawdust. Inflation eroded real wages by 30%–50%.

对无产阶级的影响：铁路瘫痪切断了燃料和粮食供应。到1916-1917年冬，彼得格勒的工厂因缺煤而关闭，导致大规模失业。城市面包供应中断。工人们不得不在零下20度的天气里排上5到6个小时的队，却只能领到混着锯末的黑面包。通货膨胀使实际工资较战前下降了30%-50%。

Specific case: In October 1916, workers in the Vyborg district went on strike. Rasputin ordered the police to disperse them with force. Cossack cavalry charged into the crowd, killing and wounding dozens, and arrested over 200, who were exiled to Siberia without proper trial.

具体案例：1916年10月，维堡区的工人举行罢工。拉斯普京下令警察以武力驱散他们。哥萨克骑兵冲入人群，造成数十人伤亡，并逮捕了200多人，这些人未经正当审判就被流放到西伯利亚。

Although Rasputin was assassinated in December 1916, the rotten government he had left behind continued into the Provisional Government period. When the February Revolution erupted, the first to rise were precisely the Petrograd workers who had been driven to desperation by bread shortages. The Bolsheviks, seizing on memories of the “Rasputin–old regime” corruption, rallied the proletariat under the slogans “Peace, Land, and Bread.”

尽管拉斯普京在1916年12月被刺杀，但他留下的腐败政府一直延续到了临时政府时期。当二月革命爆发时，最先起义的正是那些被面包短缺逼入绝境的彼得格勒工人。布尔什维克抓住人们对“拉斯普京-旧政权”腐败的记忆，在“和平、土地、面包”的口号下召集无产阶级。
